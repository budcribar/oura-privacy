Bud Health – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Bud Health is a private, personal application used by a single person (its owner) to access his own Oura Ring data through the Oura API.

Data collected

Only the owner's own Oura data: workouts, steps, calories, activity, and sleep. No data from any other person is collected.

Use

The data is used solely for the owner's personal health tracking.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored securely and are never published or shared. Access can be revoked at any time from the Oura account settings.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/budcribar/oura-privacy.
